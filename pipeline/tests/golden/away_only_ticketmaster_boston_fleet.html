About this schedule

PWHL's own schedule (the HockeyTech feed) is not used: its Terms of Use clause (xi) bans automated scripts and limits use to personal, non-commercial home use. Games below are Ticketmaster Discovery API listings for PWHL teams, not the PWHL/HockeyTech feed.

Sources and terms

Next Home Game is independent and is not affiliated with any league, team or Ticketmaster.

Game data, venues, and dates are read from the Ticketmaster Discovery API, under its Terms of Use. No league's own schedule is used on this site — each league page says why. This site does not show ticket prices. Each game's “Buy tickets” link goes to the home team's official ticket seller where we know it, and otherwise to the game's Ticketmaster listing. They are plain links, and this site is not paid for them.

This site runs no analytics, no scripts, and sets no cookies, and the calendar feeds carry no analytics either. A “Buy tickets” link goes to the ticket seller's own site and tells them we sent you. Privacy: what this site measures and what it never does.
